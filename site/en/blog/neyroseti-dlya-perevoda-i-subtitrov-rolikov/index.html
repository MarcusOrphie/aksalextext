AI for Translating and Subtitling Videos

Alex Aksenov · September 6, 2026 · 6 min read

Why subtitles are a must

A huge share of views happen with the sound off: on public transport, at work, next to a sleeping child. If a video has no subtitles, these people do not understand what it is about and leave. Text on screen holds them and noticeably boosts completion rates.

Subtitles also help those watching with sound: they hold attention, reinforce key points and make content more accessible for people who are hard of hearing. In effect, it is a free boost to any video that used to require manual work and now takes AI a minute.

Without subtitles you lose everyone who watches with the sound off, and that is the majority. Text on screen is not an option, it is the norm.

How AI creates subtitles

AI recognizes speech and turns it into timed text - each phrase appears at the right moment. All that is left for you is to check and fix. It is several times faster than typing subtitles by hand, and it is available right on your phone.

The workflow

  • Upload the video into an editor with auto-subtitles.
  • AI recognizes the speech and sets the timing.
  • Check the text: fix errors and terms.
  • Adjust the style: font, size, position.
  • Export the video with the subtitles burned in.

Checking is a must: AI makes mistakes on names, terms, rare words and poor diction. Pay especially close attention to words specific to your niche - AI often mishears them.

Translation and reaching a new audience

Translation opens your content to people who do not know your language. AI translates both subtitles and voiceover. Let us lay out the options by task so you can pick the right one.

ApproachWhat AI doesWhen it fits
Translated subtitlesTranslates the text on screenA simple entry into a language
Voiceover in the languageSynthesizes speech in another languageA full video for a new audience
Dubbing in your own voiceClones your voice in the languageKeeping your recognizability
Bilingual subtitlesShows two languages at onceEducational content

Start with translated subtitles - it is the simplest step. If the audience in the new language responds, move on to voiceover or dubbing. That way you can gradually expand your blog's geography without reshooting.

How to get a clean result

The quality of subtitles and translation starts with the quality of the original speech. The cleaner and clearer you speak, the fewer mistakes AI makes. Record in a quiet place, speak distinctly, do not rush - and there will be minimal edits.

  • Speak clearly and not too fast - recognition is more accurate.
  • Record without background noise - it confuses AI.
  • Check terms and names after transcription.
  • Keep subtitles short: one or two lines, do not cover your face.
  • For translation, check the meaning, not just the words.

Translation especially requires a check for meaning: a literal translation sometimes distorts idioms and jokes. Read the translated version through a native speaker's eyes if you can, or at least check that the idea has stayed intact.

Common mistakes with subtitles and translation

The first mistake is publishing auto-subtitles without checking them: one absurd word error catches the eye and lowers trust. The second is subtitles that are too long, taking up half the screen and covering the picture. Keep it to one or two short lines.

The third mistake is placing subtitles over a face or important details. Put them in the lower third, but not right at the edge, where the interface cuts them off. The fourth is relying on literal machine translation in sensitive topics: meaning matters more than the literal words, and it is always worth double-checking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to check auto-subtitles?

Absolutely. AI makes mistakes on names, terms, rare words and with poor diction. One absurd error in the subtitles catches the eye, so the text is always worth proofreading before publishing.

How does AI translate videos?

It translates the subtitles into another language, and it can also synthesize a voiceover and even dub in your own voice. Start with translated subtitles - it is the simplest way to enter a new language.

Why are subtitles so important?

Most people watch videos with the sound off. Without text on screen, these viewers leave. Subtitles hold them, reinforce key points and make content more accessible, noticeably boosting completion rates.

How do I get accurate subtitles?

Speak clearly and not too fast, record without background noise. The cleaner the original speech, the fewer errors AI makes. After transcription, check terms and names specific to your topic.

Can I fully trust machine translation?

For simple content, yes, but always check the meaning. A literal translation distorts idioms and jokes. In sensitive topics, reread the result and make sure the idea has stayed intact.
